High-Pressure Hydraulic Systems and Advanced Industrial Piston Pumps

In modern industrial engineering, hydraulic systems are the backbone of high-load machinery, heavy manufacturing, and automation lines. Optimizing efficiency and fluid control requires high-precision components designed to endure continuous operating pressures exceeding 350 bar.

High-Efficiency Hydraulic Pumps & Fluid Dynamics

Variable displacement axial piston pumps represent the gold standard in heavy-duty hydraulic power units. Delivering variable flow rates with minimal thermal loss, these systems enhance energy efficiency across industrial machinery.

For technical specifications, engineering consultations, and the complete catalog of high-pressure industrial hydraulic components, visit the official resource at Behfan Industrial Group.

Proportional Valve Control in Industrial Automation

Modern proportional and servo solenoid valves enable microsecond electronic fluid throttling, essential for precision robotic presses, plastic injection molding, and marine hydraulic actuators.

Industrial Cartridge Valves & Manifold Systems

Hydraulic cartridge valves (including slip-in logic elements and screw-in cartridge valves) provide modular, compact, and leak-free directional, pressure, and flow control in modern hydraulic manifolds. These components are critical for heavy-duty industrial presses, plastic machinery, and automation units where high flow rates and low pressure drops are mandatory.
